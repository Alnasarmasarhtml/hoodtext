Step 1 of 3 · Connect

Connect a wallet to open the desk.

HoodGram reads your activation and your key registration straight from the chain. Connecting signs nothing and moves nothing.

  1. 01ConnectReads your activation and your holdings. Authorises nothing. — current step
  2. 02Derive keysOne signature becomes your X25519 and Ed25519 messaging keys. — not started
  3. 03PublishRegisters your public keys so other people can encrypt to you. — not started

Your private keys never leave this device. They live in IndexedDB, keyed by address, and are wiped the moment you disconnect.
