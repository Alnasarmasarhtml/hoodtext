Pay monthly. Get paid as a holder.

HoodText runs on subscriptions, not on fees per message. Every payment is split the moment it arrives: half to the treasury that builds the product, half to whoever holds $TEXT. Holding is the whole requirement — there is no staking contract to enter and nothing to lock up.

Subscription revenue accrues to the vault continuously. Sealing turns whatever has accrued into a fixed pool and pins the block that decides who is owed what.

A share is fixed the moment an epoch seals: it depends only on your $TEXT balance at the snapshot block. Buying after the snapshot earns nothing from that epoch; selling after it changes nothing — the share is still yours.

Prices are fixed in USD on chain and converted to $TEXT at purchase.Anchors.post andGroupRegistry.createGroup are not payable — once subscribed, sending a message costs only gas. Revenue is shared by holdings through historical balance checkpoints; there is no staking, no lock-up and no deposit anywhere in this system.
