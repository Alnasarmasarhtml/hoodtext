Access & revenue

Pay $5 once. Get paid as a holder.

HoodGram charges exactly two prices, both paid in $GRAM at the live rate: $5, once, for an account that exists forever, and $10 a month for a room, paid by whoever runs it. Messages are never charged. Half of every payment goes to whoever holds $GRAM. There is no staking contract to enter and nothing to lock up.

Revenue from activations and room rents accrues to the vault continuously. Sealing turns whatever has accrued into a fixed pool and pins the block that decides who is owed what.

A share is fixed the moment an epoch seals: it depends only on your $GRAM balance at the snapshot block. Buying after the snapshot earns nothing from that epoch; selling after it changes nothing. The share is still yours.
