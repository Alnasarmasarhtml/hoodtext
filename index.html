04How a drop worksEnvelope wire format · SPEC §5

Four steps, and only one of them touches the chain.

Encryption happens on your device. The chain stores a hash, a key and a size, and that is all it ever holds.

  1. 01

    Compose

    You write a message. It is JSON on your device and it never leaves it in that form.

    client only
  2. 02

    Pad, then seal

    The payload is padded up to the smallest bucket that fits, then sealed to the recipient with a one-time ephemeral key.

    256 · 1K · 4K · 16K
  3. 03

    Anchor on chain

    One transaction records the content hash, the ephemeral key, a scan tag and the padded size. No fee is attached.

    Anchors.post — not payable
  4. 04

    Recipient scans by view tag

    Their client tests one byte against every anchor, opens the handful that match, and verifies the hash before decrypting.

    ≈1 in 256 false positives
05The noise floor4 buckets · 256 B – 16 KB

A short message and a long one leave the same trace.

Envelopes are padded to one of four fixed sizes before they are sealed. A five-character message and a five-hundred-character message leave an identical footprint.

  • 256 Bmost conversation
  • 1.0 KBoverflow
  • 4.0 KBoverflow
  • 16.0 KBlong-form ceiling

No length leak. A five-character reply and a two-hundred-character one produce byte-identical envelopes.

No recipient on chain. Anchors carry a one-byte scan tag, so a reader tests every drop cheaply without anyone learning who they are.

What this does not hide. Timing. The sequencer sees when a transaction arrives, and so does anyone reading the chain.

06Revenue shareHOLDER_BPS = 5000

50% of every subscription payment goes to holders.

Written into RevenueVault and enforced on every payment. Half of each subscription is set aside the moment it lands and paid out pro-rata by holdings.

50% · holders, by holdings50% · treasury

No staking. No lock-up. Hold $TEXT in your own wallet and you are counted at every weekly snapshot.

How an epoch pays out

  1. 01Every subscription payment goes straight into the vault, and half of it is set aside for holders.
  2. 02At least every seven days anyone can call sealEpoch(), which freezes that half against a past block — the snapshot.
  3. 03Your share is your balance at that block divided by the eligible supply, and you can claim it any time in the next 180 days.

It recurs. Every month a subscriber renews, half of that payment is set aside again, against whoever holds $TEXT at that week’s snapshot.

08QuestionsStraight answers

How it works, in plain terms.

Everything here is enforced by the contracts and the client, and every claim is something you can verify yourself.

What exactly is encrypted?
Everything you write. Messages are sealed on your device with X25519 key agreement and XSalsa20-Poly1305 before they touch the network, and the recipient’s key is the only key that opens them.
Who can read my messages?
The person you sent them to. That is the complete list. The relay only ever holds sealed bytes and the chain only ever holds a hash of them.
What does the chain actually store?
A 32-byte content hash, a one-time public key, a one-byte scan tag and a padded size bucket. That record proves a message happened and when, and it is the entire public footprint.
Can anyone tell how long my messages are?
Every envelope is padded to one of four fixed sizes before it is sealed, so a five-character message and a five-hundred-character message leave an identical footprint on chain.
Where do my keys live?
On your device. One signature from your wallet derives them, they are cached in IndexedDB keyed to your address, and they are wiped the moment you disconnect. They are never transmitted anywhere.
Are messages permanent?
Yes. Every anchor is a permanent public receipt on Robinhood Chain, so your history can be proven, verified and independently reconstructed for as long as the chain exists.
What happens if my subscription lapses?
You keep everything. Your keys, your full history and the ability to read and receive all stay exactly where they are. Renew whenever you like and start posting again the same second.
Am I charged per message?
Your $25 a month buys unlimited messages. Sending costs about a cent of gas, paid to the network, and Anchors.post is not even payable.
Do I have to stake to earn the revenue share?
Hold $TEXT in your own wallet and you are counted at every weekly snapshot. The token records balance history on chain, so your share is calculated from what you already hold and you claim it whenever you want.
What happens if I change tier mid-month?
Your remaining time keeps its full dollar value and converts to the new tier at that tier’s rate. Renewing early always adds time on top of what you already own.
Where does the $TEXT price come from?
Tier prices are fixed in dollars on chain and converted to $TEXT at the moment you buy, so a $25 tier stays a $25 tier while the token moves. The IPriceSource interface lets a Uniswap TWAP take over the rate as the pool deepens.
